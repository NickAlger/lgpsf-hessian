\begin{center}\textbf{The deflation correction as implemented}\\[2pt]
\small The formulas behind \texttt{lgh\_glr\_correct} /
\texttt{lgh\_glr\_correct\_probes} (stage 2.5 of \texttt{lgpsf-hessian}).
Companions: \texttt{glr-woodbury-formulas.pdf} (the base representation),
\texttt{glr-distributed-design.pdf} (layouts).
\end{center}

Definitions: $M$ lumped mass (diagonal); $Z$ the prior's square-root factor,
prior precision $R = Z M^{-1} Z$; $H_d$ the \emph{true} misfit Hessian
(matvecs only); $\widetilde{H}_d$ the sparse lgpsf approximation; the GLR
build holds $U \Lambda U^T \approx F := M^{1/2} Z^{-1} \widetilde{H}_d
Z^{-1} M^{1/2}$ (treated, truncated, $U^T U = I_r$).  The approximated and
true operators at shift $c$:
\begin{align}
\widetilde{H}(c) &= Z M^{-1/2}\,(U \Lambda U^T + cI)\,M^{-1/2} Z, &
H(c) &= H_d + c R = Z M^{-1/2}\,(F_{\mathrm{true}} + cI)\,M^{-1/2} Z,
\end{align}
with $F_{\mathrm{true}} := M^{1/2} Z^{-1} H_d Z^{-1} M^{1/2}$ (never formed;
one true apply per column).

\section*{1. The error eigenvalue problem}

At a reference shift $c_0 > 0$, the correction targets the dominant
eigenpairs of the generalized problem
\begin{equation}
\bigl(H(c_0) - \widetilde{H}(c_0)\bigr)\, v \;=\; d\; \widetilde{H}(c_0)\, v ,
\qquad H(c_0) - \widetilde{H}(c_0) = H_d - \widetilde{H}_d^{\,\mathrm{glr}}
\ \ (\text{the regularization cancels}).
\label{eq:gevp}
\end{equation}
In prior-whitened coordinates, with
$S^{\pm} := (U \Lambda U^T + c_0 I)^{\pm 1/2}$ (one filter apply each via
the master formula,
$S^{\pm} = U\bigl[(\Lambda + c_0 I)^{\pm 1/2} - c_0^{\pm 1/2} I\bigr]U^T
+ c_0^{\pm 1/2} I$, i.e.\ \texttt{lgh\_glr\_pow}$(c_0, \pm\tfrac12)$),
\eqref{eq:gevp} is the ordinary symmetric problem for the whitened error
operator
\begin{equation}
E \;=\; S^-\,(F_{\mathrm{true}} + c_0 I)\,S^- \;-\; I
   \;=\; S^-\,\bigl(F_{\mathrm{true}} - U \Lambda U^T\bigr)\,S^- .
\label{eq:Ew}
\end{equation}
Facts used by the implementation: (i) the eigenvalues $d$ of $E$ are those of
\eqref{eq:gevp} (congruence); (ii)
$\mathrm{eig}\bigl(\widetilde{H}^{-1} H\bigr) = 1 + d$; (iii) if $H(c_0)$ is
PD then $1 + d > 0$ exactly, so any Rayleigh estimate below
$-1+\varepsilon$ is estimation noise and is clamped there
(\texttt{clamp\_eps}, default $0.05$; clamps are counted, not hidden).

\section*{2. The correction and its $c$-independence}

With $(V, D)$ the kept eigenpairs of $E$ ($V^T V = I$, $D$ diagonal,
$|d|$-descending, clamped),
\begin{equation}
F_{\mathrm{true}} \;\approx\; U \Lambda U^T + \widehat{V} D \widehat{V}^T,
\qquad \widehat{V} := S^{+} V,
\label{eq:corr}
\end{equation}
and hence, for \emph{every} shift $c$ (the stored object approximates the
$c$-free error $F_{\mathrm{true}} - U\Lambda U^T$; $c_0$ enters only as the
metric selecting which modes dominate),
\begin{equation}
H(c) \;\approx\; Z M^{-1/2} \bigl(U \Lambda U^T
  + \widehat{V} D \widehat{V}^T + cI\bigr) M^{-1/2} Z .
\end{equation}

\section*{3. Value pass (probe reuse; \texttt{lgh\_glr\_correct\_probes})}

Given the fit stage's probe pairs $(\Omega,\ Y = H_d \Omega)$, $k_f$ columns
after holding out the trailing $n_{qc}$:
\begin{align}
X &= M^{-1/2} Z^T \Omega \quad (\text{cheap applies}), &
F_{\mathrm{true}} X &= M^{1/2} Z^{-1} Y \quad (\text{blocked $Z$ solves}),\\
R_w &= F_{\mathrm{true}} X - U \Lambda U^T X, &
E\,(S^{+} X) &= S^- R_w \quad (\text{zero new true applies}).
\end{align}
An \emph{energy-ordered} orthonormal basis of the free images: eigendecompose
the Gram matrix $G = (S^- R_w)^T (S^- R_w) = W \Sigma W^T$, drop
$\sigma_i \le \texttt{drop\_tol}\cdot\sigma_{\max}$, set
$Q = (S^- R_w)\, W_+ \Sigma_+^{-1/2}$ (columns in descending energy).
The apply budget $m$ then prices directions exactly:
\begin{equation}
T \;=\; \mathrm{sym}\bigl(Q_m^T\, E\, Q_m\bigr), \qquad
E Q_m = S^-\bigl((F_{\mathrm{true}} + c_0 I)(S^- Q_m)\bigr) - Q_m
\quad (\text{$m$ true applies}),
\end{equation}
$T = V_s D V_s^T$, $V = Q_m V_s$.  If budget remains beyond the basis
dimension, one power step: orthogonalize $E Q_m$ against $Q_m$, take the
energy basis of the remainder, price it with the remaining applies, and
assemble $T$ on the combined block.

\section*{4. Fresh sketch (\texttt{lgh\_glr\_correct})}

Without probe data: a hashed Gaussian test block $\Omega_E$
($m_r = \lfloor m / (2 + q) \rfloor$ columns), images $E\,\Omega_E$
($m_r$ true applies per pass, $q$ optional power passes through the energy
basis), then the same Rayleigh pricing on the final basis --- $\approx 2m_r$
applies for a rank-$m_r$ correction.

\section*{5. Graft}

The correction is folded into the representation, after which every
downstream operation (solve, apply, sample, factor, logdet, pow, filter)
serves the corrected operator through the unchanged master formula:
\begin{align}
C_U &= U^T \widehat{V}, \qquad
Q_V = \mathrm{orth}_{\mathrm{energy}}\bigl((I - U U^T)\widehat{V}\bigr), \qquad
C_Q = Q_V^T \widehat{V},\\
T_2 &= \begin{pmatrix} \Lambda + C_U D C_U^T & C_U D C_Q^T\\
                       C_Q D C_U^T & C_Q D C_Q^T \end{pmatrix}
     = W_2 \Lambda' W_2^T \qquad (\text{dense symmetric, } r + r_2),\\
U' &= [\,U \ \ Q_V\,]\, W_2 \quad (\text{truncated by } |\lambda'| \text{ with
the object's rules; \textbf{signed} --- no treatment}).
\end{align}
Validity of shift-taking operations becomes the exact scalar condition
\begin{equation}
c \;>\; \mathrm{floor} \;=\; \max(0,\ -\min_i \lambda'_i)
\qquad (\text{reported; } \mathrm{floor} < c_0 \text{ guaranteed by the
clamp}).
\end{equation}
After a graft the sketch no longer represents the operator:
\texttt{lgh\_glr\_extend} is refused (order: compute $\to$ extend $\to$
correct last), \texttt{lgh\_glr\_apply} switches from the exact sparse
action to the corrected low-rank action, and ScaLAPACK-built objects first
materialize the implicit $U = Q V_{\mathrm{kept}}$ (panel-wise) and drop the
sketch state.

Per column of width-$m$ blocks; ``true apply'' means one $H_d$ matvec
(typically a forward $+$ adjoint PDE solve).
